title: Convert emoji to SVG
date: 2026-08-11 20:35:56
excerpt: How to convert an emoji to SVG.
categories: emoji svg
---

Copy your [emoji](https://remarkablemark.org/emoji/) and replace it with 👋:

```xml
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 100 100">
  <text y=".9em" font-size="90">👋</text>
</svg>
```

Then you can convert the [SVG to an image](https://remarkablemark.org/svg-to-image/).
